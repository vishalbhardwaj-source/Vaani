# Acoustic Evaluation Participant Consent Record

**Study Title:** Real Telephony Acoustic Robustness Evaluation for Indic Logistics Dispatch  
**Protocol:** Spoken telephonic audio recording across real acoustic environments (mobile handsets, telephony codecs, ambient roadside and household noise).  
**Repository:** Vaani Voice AI Logistics Dispatcher  
**Date of Collection:** November 2026  

---

## 1. Ethical Guidelines & Data Privacy
- **Voluntary Participation:** All participants volunteered to read standardized logistics dispatch utterances over standard GSM mobile telephony / VoIP calls.
- **Anonymization:** No personal identification numbers, names, phone numbers, or biographical data are stored in this repository. Participants are referenced solely by non-identifying keys (`SPK_A` through `SPK_E`).
- **Scope of Use:** Spoken audio clips are recorded solely for evaluating automatic speech recognition (ASR) error rates on critical numerical values (temperatures, distances) in noisy telephonic environments.
- **Biometric Protection:** In compliance with ethical research standards and contest privacy safeguards, voice recordings are evaluated locally or via secure API, and no identifiable biometrics are published publicly.

## 3. Acoustic Environment Breakdown
1. **Street & Market Ambient (`SPK_D`):** 60-70 dBA ambient background noise; vehicular horns, passing traffic, crowd chatter.
2. **Two-Wheeler / Roadside (`SPK_A`):** Wind buffet, engine rumble, mobile handset compression.
3. **Kitchen with Exhaust Fan (`SPK_C`):** High-frequency white noise, acoustic reverberation, 65 dBA continuous background.
4. **Enclosed Cabin (`SPK_B`):** GSM cellular codec degradation, cabin resonance, low-frequency engine idle.
5. **Quiet Baseline (`SPK_E`):** Clean cellular connection for baseline telephonic codec degradation measurement.
